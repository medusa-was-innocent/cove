How a room works

What is a room?
A room is just a shared URL. Open it, name yourself, and anyone else with that link appears in the grid. There is no calendar, no lobby attendant, no host key.
How many people can join?
Cove uses a mesh: every person connects directly to every other person. That's why it's private and low-latency — and why it feels best around six to eight people on a decent connection.
Do you see my camera?
No. Media never touches our servers. We only pass the tiny handshake that lets browsers find each other. Chat stays on the devices in the room.
What if I don't have a camera?
Join anyway. You'll show as a name tile, you can still hear and type, and you can turn a camera on later if one appears.
